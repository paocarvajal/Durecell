Duracell LATAM Expansion

Knowledge Capture & Transition Process - Functional View

1. Project Overview & Refined Phasing

This updated infographic provides a clear, function-specific view of the Knowledge Capture (KC) and Knowledge Transfer (KT) process for Duracell's new Latin America operations. Based on recent discussions, we've refined the project phasing to align with internal milestones and deliverables, clarifying the distinction between our "Go Live" and the client's "Observe/Assist/Execute" nomenclature. The focus is on a structured approach by functional stream, ensuring comprehensive knowledge transfer and readiness for production work.

2. Key Roles & Responsibilities

Duracell Business Team

Owns business processes, provides SMEs, creates Detailed Process Templates (DTPs), and validates captured knowledge. Key in the 'Observe' phase.

PwC Consulting Team

Facilitates methodology, assesses DTPs, guides KT, and oversees training. Supports 'Assist' and 'Execute' phases.

IT & Technology Team

Manages KC platform (SharePoint), communication tools (Teams), and system readiness. Crucial for enabling all phases with technology.

3. Aligning on Nomenclature: Our Phases vs. Client's View

A key point of alignment is the project phasing and the definition of "Go Live." Our internal project plan utilizes distinct phases with specific deliverables, which we need to clearly map against the client's operational terminology.

Our Project Phases & Milestones

Planning

Milestone: Transition Plan

Knowledge Capture (KC)

Milestone: Finalized DTPs

Knowledge Transfer (KT)

Milestone: Readiness for Production Work

Service Rehearsal (Ramp to Volume)

Milestone: Full Service Rehearsal & Go-Live Report Issued

Hypercare

Milestone: 30-60-90 Day Plan Executed

Our Go Live

Occurs *after* Service Rehearsal

Client's Operational View

Observe

Initial phase, understanding current state.

Assist

Providing support and guidance.

Execute

Performing the actual work.

Client's Go Live

Occurs *after* Execute phase
